DROP YOUR SCRAPBOOK PHOTOS IN THIS FOLDER

Supported formats:
  .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp, .gif, .avif

Before hosting:
1. Copy your photos into this folder.
2. Run the normal build command: pnpm build
3. Deploy the generated dist folder.

The build automatically finds every supported image in this folder and adds it
to the scrapbook gallery. Number filenames (01-us.jpg, 02-date-night.jpg) to
control their display order.

The photos added with the website's "Add our photos" button are browser-local
and private to that device. Photos placed in this folder are included in the
hosted site and visible to her too.
